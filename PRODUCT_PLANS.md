# ChargeReady product decisions

- Free requires registration for saved applications and contains basic details, family and housing plans; no PDF.
- Complete costs USD 5 per application. Full intake, job-application ledger, evidence/consistency checklist, practice prompts, optional wording help, saved revisions and branded 2–3 page PDF are included, plus five successful AI preparation reviews when connected. Extra packs add five reviews for USD 5. Failed reviews are refunded automatically in the credit ledger; no automatic extra charge occurs. Corrections and downloads for the same application are included; new applications start Free.
- The app review and PDF share one presentation model and schema labels. Facts, calculations, dates and evidence references must match. No invented source verification, promises of coverage or approval predictions.
- Value comes from personalized organization, evidence gaps, realistic planning, clear presentation and reliable saved work. Question secrecy cannot prevent paid customers from copying what they see.
- Optional grammar AI is advertised as available only when configured. It is editing, not fact checking. Users approve highlighted changes. Basic spacing cleanup works offline from AI.
- Advertising may state encrypted account backups, password hashing and the implemented session/MFA controls. Never claim certified/bank-level security, zero operator access, government endorsement, legal advice or guaranteed approval. Server-side validation/PDF processing temporarily receives plaintext. PDFs/recovery-code downloads are unencrypted.
- The initial security notice must be acknowledged. Private support replaces public issue submission. Business identity, approved policies and independent review are prerequisites for real launch.
- The fictional teaching profile remains separate and read-only; user data and source immigration packages are not distributed.

Future value opportunities, requiring separate validation: provider-ready insurance quote comparison worksheets with explicit dates/status, a translation-needed evidence checklist, interview-day binder labels, and consent-based reminder emails. Do not invent quotes, send outreach, schedule reminders or publish automatically.

The interface uses simple English only. Visa code lookup includes 159 choices from the published State Department reference, including a separate EB-2 NIW label; no invented EB-3 NIW option. The first interview and follow-up interview both work without any date or embassy request. Advanced provenance and generated document IDs do not block the core guided questions. Working brand: ChargeReady, with a matching document logo; domain/trademark clearance remains a launch task.

Preparation completeness is a transparent checklist score, never an approval/eligibility/risk score. It does not reward wealth, younger age or better health. AI explains next steps; the applicant remains responsible for factual statements. AI notes/scores stay outside the printed fact summary.
