Privacy, use and refunds

Local-preview policy draft · October 17, 2026. Operator details and independent legal, immigration-content and security review are required before public launch. This page does not claim legal compliance or security certification.

What this tool does

ChargeReady helps applicants organize their own dated facts and evidence. It is not government-affiliated, a law firm, legal advice, an official worksheet or a guarantee of approval. Use this tool before a first interview or any follow-up interview. Follow current official instructions and any specific embassy request. Applicants review and remain responsible for their statements.

Free and Complete

Free includes your basic details, family and housing plans. Complete is USD 5 for one application, including corrections, saved revisions, PDF re-downloads and five successful AI preparation reviews when the AI service is connected. Five extra successful AI reviews cost USD 5 for the same application. The ordinary preparation checklist does not use AI credits. A new application starts Free and needs its own purchase. Payment never changes an immigration outcome. The local preview does not charge unless a real provider is explicitly configured.

Storage and processing

Account dossier copies and revisions are encrypted in your browser before storage. Your login password and an offline recovery code separately protect a random account encryption key. Passwords are hashed; session cookies are HttpOnly and expire. Validation, consistency checks and PDF creation temporarily process the submitted answers on the server. PDF page preview rendering uses a private temporary directory that is removed after the request. This is not a claim that the operator cannot access data being processed, or that compromised devices are protected.

PDF downloads are unencrypted. Never enter passport numbers, Social Security numbers, full bank-account numbers or reused passwords. Use aliases and write private identifiers into the blank printed fields yourself. Evidence documents are indexed but not uploaded or verified.

Optional AI

Only the answer, section or full profile that you explicitly permit is sent to the configured AI provider. A full-profile review shares financial, family, work, benefits, housing, support and insurance answers; removing display name and embassy does not make the profile anonymous. Completed AI explanations are encrypted on the server with its private encryption key so you can read them later. They are protected by owner-only account access; this is different from browser-encrypted dossier backups. The server retains completion/usage metadata and a keyed profile fingerprint for replay protection. Failed requests do not use a credit. Preparation scores use a published checklist formula, not visa eligibility or approval probabilities. Provider retention rules apply. Suggestions are not fact verification; applicants approve wording before it changes. Basic spacing cleanup does not use AI.

Payment and support records

PayPal handles payment credentials when configured; ChargeReady stores order, amount, currency and provider references for reconciliation, not card numbers. Private support tickets are visible to the applicant and support administrators. Do not include financial, immigration, medical, password or recovery-code details in tickets.

Retention, deletion and recovery

The local preview retains up to 20 encrypted revisions per application. Resetting an application saves a new blank revision. Closing an account removes encrypted applicant records, AI review results, keys, support messages and the email identity. Minimal deidentified transaction and audit records remain; the production operator must approve and publish retention periods, backup expiry and deletion procedures before launch. Downloaded files on your devices remain your responsibility.

Keep your private recovery code offline. Password reset preserves encrypted answers only when you can unlock the data key with that recovery code. Without the password or recovery code, support cannot recreate the encrypted contents. Authenticator recovery for administrators is an owner-controlled local procedure, not a public MFA bypass.

Refunds and delivery problems

If a duplicate charge, failed access or document-delivery error occurs, open a private Payment or PDF support ticket before paying again. Include only an order reference and the problem. The operator must review the provider transaction and communicate the resolution. Recording a refund in the admin console does not itself transfer money; the operator must issue any approved refund through the payment provider. A final jurisdiction-appropriate refund policy and operator contact details must be reviewed and published before collecting public payments.

Cookies, logs and analytics

A first-party session cookie supports sign-in. A session-storage entry remembers the notice version for this tab; signed-in notice acknowledgement is also recorded on the account. Rate limiting temporarily stores hashed request subjects, not raw IP addresses. Operational analytics use application counts and completion metadata. An optional, self-reported signup country / region is stored as account metadata and used for aggregate business trends; you may choose not to provide it. This version does not infer location from your IP or record detailed financial trends. Server errors use reference IDs without logging answers or credentials.
